Privacy & security

How we handle personal data, video footage and site records — and the controls that sit behind the platform.

Last updated: 11 August 2026

Template — not yet legally reviewed. This page is a structural draft written to fit the site. Before launch it must be replaced with a notice reviewed by counsel for every jurisdiction you operate in (India's DPDP Act and the GDPR both apply to a business selling into these markets).

Who we are

mySecurity Technologies (“mySecurity”, “we”) provides AI security software and edge hardware to residential and commercial customers. For enquiries about this notice, write to privacy@mysecurity.ai.

Our role depends on the context. For our website and sales enquiries we are the data controller. For footage and access records generated on a customer's site, the customer is the controller and we act as their data processor under a written agreement.

What we collect on this website

  • Enquiry details you type into our forms: name, email, phone, organisation, property type, camera count and your message.
  • Basic request data logged by our hosting provider: IP address, user agent and requested page, retained for a short period for security and abuse prevention.

This website does not use advertising cookies or third-party analytics trackers. The only third-party request the site makes is to Google Fonts to load typefaces.

What the product processes on your site

  • Video and images from cameras you connect.
  • Access records: badge, PIN, plate, QR and biometric events, where you have enabled them.
  • Visitor records: name, contact details, host, purpose and time of visit.
  • Sensor readings from environment and asset devices.
  • Operator activity: who viewed, exported, overrode or acknowledged what, and when.

Recognition processing runs on the edge appliance located at your premises. In the default hybrid deployment, continuous video is not transmitted off site; only event metadata and any clips you explicitly share or retain in the cloud console leave the property.

Lawful basis and consent

Where we act as controller, we rely on legitimate interests for responding to enquiries and on consent where you have given it. Where you deploy mySecurity on your own site, you are responsible for having a lawful basis for the monitoring you configure — including signage, employee notification, works-council consultation where required, and consent for any biometric processing.

Retention

  • Sales enquiries: 24 months from last contact, unless you ask us to erase them sooner.
  • Site event metadata: the window set in your plan, configurable per site.
  • Video clips: your configured retention window, with legal hold available per incident.
  • Audit logs: retained for the life of the contract and a defined period afterwards.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, erase, restrict or port your personal data, and to object to certain processing. Contact privacy@mysecurity.ai and we will respond within the statutory period. If the data sits on a customer's site, we will route your request to that customer as the controller and support them in answering it.

Security controls

  • In transit: TLS 1.3 between appliance, console and clients.
  • At rest: AES-256 with per-site key isolation.
  • Access: role-based, least-privilege, with mandatory MFA for administrative roles and SSO available on enterprise plans.
  • Support access: mySecurity staff have no standing access to customer footage. Support sessions are time-boxed, customer-approved and written to the customer's own audit log.
  • Isolation: each customer's data is logically separated; private-cloud and on-premise deployments are physically separated.
  • Testing: independent penetration testing on an annual cycle, with remediation tracked to closure.
  • Disclosure: report a vulnerability to security@mysecurity.ai. We aim to acknowledge within one business day.

Sub-processors and transfers

We use a small number of sub-processors for hosting, email and error reporting. A current list is available on request and is maintained as an annex to the customer data-processing agreement. Where data crosses borders, transfers are covered by appropriate safeguards; customers who need data to stay in a specific country can pin residency or deploy fully on-premise.

Changes

We will post material changes to this notice here and, for customers, notify the named contact on the account before the change takes effect.
