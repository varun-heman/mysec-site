Day to day

What it actually changes.

Not a feature list — the specific moments where a house with mySecurity behaves differently from a house with cameras.

The gate opens for you

Your cars, your parents' cars and the regular help are recognised on approach. Everyone else has to be let in — by a person, from a phone, wherever they are.

Guests let themselves in

Send a pass by message. It works for the window you set, opens the gate they need, and expires on its own. No shared codes, no keys under the mat.

You know when the kids are home

A quiet notification when school pickup arrives, when the help leaves, when a delivery is dropped — without a camera feed anyone has to sit and watch.

Safety zones around risk

Pool edges, driveways, terraces and stairwells can be watched for small children and elderly relatives. It's the one alert nobody minds getting.

Deliveries don't disappear

The system sees the parcel arrive, sees it sit, and sees who picks it up. If it leaves with someone who didn't deliver it, you have the clip before you notice it's gone.

Away mode that means it

Travelling? The tolerance narrows, the escalation shortens, and a person on your list gets called — not just a push notification into a different time zone.

Gated communities & RWAs

One perimeter, hundreds of households.

Community security fails at the seams: the visitor register nobody fills in, the gate held open for a queue, the guard who just started yesterday. mySecurity closes those seams with process, not effort.

  • Resident vehicle registry with automatic barrier entry and exit
  • Digital visitor register — searchable, exportable, no paper book
  • Domestic staff and vendor attendance with in/out timestamps
  • Per-block dashboards for the committee, per-gate views for guards
  • Monthly security report generated automatically for the RWA meeting
  • Emergency broadcast to every household from one screen

Privacy at home

A camera in your home is a serious thing.

We treat it that way. These aren't settings buried three menus deep — they're how the product is built.

Processing stays in the house

Recognition runs on the appliance in your utility cupboard. Continuous video never needs to leave your property.

Indoor cameras are opt-in, room by room

And they can be scheduled off whenever the family is home, automatically.

Neighbours are masked

Privacy zones cover their windows, gardens and the public pavement before a frame is ever stored.

You control who can look

Household members, your guard agency and nobody else. Every view is logged, including ours.
