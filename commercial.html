By sector

Different sites, different failure modes.

The engine is the same. The defaults, zones and playbooks are not.

Tailgating & badge sharing

Cameras above every reader count bodies against credentials and flag mismatches while the person is still in the lobby.

Reception without a receptionist

Pre-registered visitors self check-in, hosts are notified, and NDAs or inductions are collected before the badge prints.

Sensitive rooms

Server rooms, HR floors and labs get dual authorisation, camera-confirmed entry and automatic after-hours reporting.

Shrinkage patterns

Concealment behaviour, repeat-visitor patterns and till-area anomalies, correlated with POS voids rather than guessed at.

Queue & occupancy

Live counts by zone, queue-length alerts to the floor manager, and footfall reporting that doesn't need a separate vendor.

Open and close

Verified opening and closing procedures — doors, tills, shutters, alarm set — with an exception report, not a checklist nobody reads.

Yard & dock control

ANPR at the gate matches the vehicle to a booking, assigns a bay, and times the turnaround without a clipboard.

Load verification

Camera evidence of what went onto which truck, tied to the dispatch record, ready for the inevitable dispute.

Forklift & pedestrian safety

Segregation-zone breaches, speed in aisles and near-miss events logged as safety data, not just security incidents.

PPE compliance

Helmet, vest and boundary checks at the point of entry to each area — a nudge at the door beats a report next month.

Process & utility rooms

Temperature, pressure and pump state alongside the cameras, so an environmental fault and its footage arrive together.

Lockout / tagout

Restricted-area entry during maintenance windows is verified against the permit, and unauthorised entry stops the line.

Audit & evidence

Prove it, don't just claim it.

Insurers, regulators and clients increasingly ask for evidence rather than assurances. Everything mySecurity does is recorded in a form you can hand over.

Every incident produces a single exportable record: the triggering signal, the clips, the confidence, the policy that fired, who was notified, who acknowledged, what action was taken, and how long each step took. Hash-chained so it can't be quietly edited later.

Who had access to what, when it was granted, by whom, and when it was revoked — including access that was granted temporarily and expired on its own. Standing-access reviews can be scheduled and signed off in the console.

PPE checks, restricted-zone breaches, permit-to-work verification and near-miss events roll up into monthly reports per site and per contractor, with the underlying clips retained for your defined retention period.

Retention windows are set per camera class and per site, with legal hold available on specific incidents. Deletion is verifiable, and export requests are logged like any other access. Data residency can be pinned to a region or kept entirely on-premise.
