    One system

    Everything on site, under one intelligence.

    Most properties run five disconnected systems and a WhatsApp group. mySecurity puts cameras, access control, visitors, assets and emergencies behind a single brain — and a single screen.

    Video intelligence

    Every camera gets a description, not just a motion box. People, vehicles, packages, loitering, tailgating, crowding, fallen persons, open doors — recognised in context and searchable in plain English.

    Gates & access control

    Boom barriers, sliding gates, turnstiles and door controllers driven by policy rather than a guard's judgement. Number-plate recognition, resident lists, delivery windows and one-tap remote open.

    Visitors & guests

    Pre-authorised passes, QR check-in, ID capture and automatic host notification. Every entry and exit is logged with a face, a time and a reason — and expiring passes close themselves.

    Asset & environment monitoring

    Watch the things that quietly cost you money: cold-chain temperature, generator fuel, pump rooms, high-value stock, server racks. Thresholds, drift detection and an alert before it's a loss.

    Emergency management

    Fire, smoke, gas, medical and intrusion events trigger a rehearsed playbook: unlock egress, sound the right zone, call the right people in the right order, and hold an evidence-grade timeline.

    Perimeter & intrusion

    Virtual tripwires, fence-line analytics, loitering zones and after-hours rules. The system learns what your normal looks like, so a cat on the wall doesn't wake the whole street.

    How it works

    Connect. Understand. Decide. Act.

    Read the platform detail
    Step 01

    Connect

    An on-site edge appliance discovers your existing cameras over ONVIF/RTSP and speaks to your gate, reader and alarm hardware. Nothing gets ripped out.

    Step 02

    Understand

    Vision and sensor models build a continuous description of the site: who is here, what moved, what is open, what is out of range — and what counts as normal for this place.

    Step 03

    Decide

    Your rules and escalation ladder run against that understanding. Routine things are handled silently. Anything ambiguous is verified before a human is disturbed.

    Step 04

    Act

    Open the gate, lock the door, sound a zone, call the guard, notify the owner, dispatch a responder — and record every step as a time-stamped, exportable trail.

    Privacy & control

    Your footage stays yours.

    A security system that leaks is not a security system. Recognition runs on the edge appliance inside your building; only events, not continuous video, ever need to leave the site.

    • Edge-first processing. Video is analysed locally. Cloud is for coordination, not surveillance.
    • Encrypted end to end. TLS in transit, AES-256 at rest, per-site key isolation.
    • Role-based access. Guards, managers, owners and auditors each see only their slice.
    • Full audit trail. Every view, export, override and door-open is attributed and immutable.
    • Data residency you choose. Keep records in-country, or entirely on-premise.

    Runs offline

    Loses internet? Detection, access decisions and local sirens keep working. Events sync when the link returns.

    Least privilege

    Time-bound access, approval workflows for exports, and automatic revocation when someone leaves.

    Privacy zones

    Mask neighbours' windows, changing rooms and pavements. Redaction is enforced before the frame is stored.

    Explainable alerts

    Every alert carries the clip, the reasoning and the confidence — so you can challenge it, not just trust it.
